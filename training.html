Exercise #01 · Front & side delts · triceps

Shoulder press

Build strong, powerful shoulders

How to perform

  1. Sit tall with dumbbells at shoulder level.
  2. Brace your core and press upward.
  3. Lower slowly with control.

Sets & reps

3–4 sets8–12 reps

Pro tip

Avoid excessive back arching. Keep your core tight and use a controlled range of motion.

Exercise #02 · Side delts

Lateral raises

Build wider, rounder shoulders

How to perform

  1. Stand tall with dumbbells by your sides.
  2. Raise your arms out to the sides.
  3. Stop around shoulder height and lower slowly.

Sets & reps

3–4 sets12–15 reps

Pro tip

Use lighter weights and controlled reps. Avoid swinging your body to lift the dumbbells.

Exercise #03 · Rear delts · upper back

Rear delt fly

Build stronger, more complete shoulders

How to perform

  1. Hinge forward with a flat, neutral back.
  2. Raise dumbbells out to the sides.
  3. Squeeze your rear delts and lower slowly.

Sets & reps

3–4 sets12–15 reps

Pro tip

Keep your torso stable and avoid using momentum to swing the weights.

Mistake #01 · Chest day fix

Bad bench setup

Your chest workout starts before the first rep

The fix

  1. Plant your feet firmly on the floor.
  2. Pull your shoulder blades back and down.
  3. Keep your upper back stable with a natural arch.

Remember

Feet plantedShoulders set

Pro tip

Set your shoulders before unracking. Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows.

Mistake #02 · Chest day fix

Bouncing the weight

Control the rep — don’t let momentum take over

The fix

  1. Lower the bar under control.
  2. Keep your torso and shoulders stable.
  3. Press smoothly without bouncing off your chest.

Remember

Slow downOwn the rep

Pro tip

Use a manageable weight that lets you control every rep.

Mistake #03 · Chest day fix

Poor range of motion

Half reps can limit your chest training

The fix

  1. Lower the bar with control.
  2. Use a comfortable, pain-free range of motion.
  3. Press back up without losing your setup.

Remember

Full controlComfortable range

Pro tip

Don’t force excessive depth. Your range should suit your mobility and shoulder comfort.

Things you don’t need to get fit

You needconsistency.

01

Expensive supplements

Supplements can help. They’re not a requirement — food, training and consistency come first.

02

A perfect diet

You don’t need to eat “clean” 100% of the time. Enough protein, balanced meals, reasonable portions.

03

2-hour workouts

You don’t need to live in the gym. 45–60 minutes of focused training can be enough. Train, recover, repeat.
