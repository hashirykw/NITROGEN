The Nitrogen way

5 checks.Better reps.

Most people pull with their arms, not their back. Our coaches run every new member through the same checklist before the weight goes up.

  1. Chest upKeep your chest proud and lifted.
  2. Shoulders downDon’t let them creep toward your ears.
  3. Elbows drive downLead with your elbows, not your hands.
  4. No swingingKeep your torso stable and controlled.
  5. Control the returnDon’t just let the weight pull you up.
